### Video 3: "What is JSON-LD structured data and why does it matter for AI search?"
**Why:** This is the technical education video. It's the one that proves you know what you're talking about.
**Length:** 8-12 minutes

**Talking points:**
- JSON-LD = JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data. It's a machine-readable label that tells AI what your page is about
- Show a real example: go to a site with structured data, open DevTools, show the JSON-LD
- Compare: same site without structured data - the AI sees a wall of HTML and can't tell what's a business name, what's an address, what's a FAQ
- The 5 schema types that matter most: LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Organization, BreadcrumbList, OpeningHours
- How AI uses it: when someone asks "what's the best dentist in Columbus?", the AI reads LocalBusiness schema on every dentist site and synthesizes an answer
- Without schema: you're invisible. With schema: you're the cited source.
