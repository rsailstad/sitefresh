## SECTION 2: What speeds it up (2:30 - 4:00)

[Camera on]

Some sites get found faster. Here's what speeds up the process:

**Fast page speed.** AI crawlers prioritize fast-loading pages. If your site loads in under two seconds, the crawler reads it quickly and moves on. If it takes five seconds, the crawler might skip it and come back later. This is why we build sites under 50KB total.

**Google Business Profile.** If you have a Google Business Profile that links to your website, Google's crawler finds your site faster. It's a direct signal. Claim your profile, add your URL, and Google knows your site exists immediately.

**Existing backlinks.** If other sites already link to yours - directories, review sites, news articles - the crawlers follow those links and find your new structured data faster.

**XML sitemap.** We include a sitemap with every site we build. It's a file that tells crawlers "here are all my pages, here's when they were last updated." It's like a map for the AI.

**Frequently asked questions.** The more FAQ content you have on your site, the more questions the AI can answer from you. Twenty FAQ entries gives the AI twenty potential reasons to cite you. Five gives it five. More questions equals more chances to be the answer.

---

## SECTION 3: What slows it down (4:00 - 5:00)

[Camera on]

And here's what slows it down:

**No structured data.** If your site has no JSON-LD, the AI can't parse it. It might read the text content, but it can't understand the structure. It won't cite you as a source because it can't verify what you are.

**Slow page load.** Heavy sites with 300KB of JavaScript and render-blocking scripts get crawled less frequently. The AI deprioritizes them.

**Duplicate or thin content.** If your FAQ answers are the same as every other site in your industry - "We provide excellent service" - the AI doesn't see value in citing you. Specific, detailed answers get cited. Generic ones don't.

**No updates.** If your site hasn't been updated in two years, the AI assumes the information might be stale. Fresh content - updated FAQ, new schema, current pricing - signals that your site is alive and accurate.
