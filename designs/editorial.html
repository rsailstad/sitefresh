What is AI search optimization?
AI search optimization means structuring your website so that AI search engines - Google SGE, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, and others - can read it, understand it, and cite it as an authoritative source. This includes JSON-LD structured data (FAQ schema, service schema, business schema), semantic HTML, Open Graph tags, and clean content that answers real questions. It’s the 2026 version of SEO, designed for how people actually search now.

What is structured data / schema?
Structured data is code embedded in your website that tells search engines exactly what your content means - not just what it says. We use JSON-LD (the format Google recommends) to mark up your business info, services, FAQ, and hours. When Google SGE, ChatGPT, or Perplexity crawls your site, they can read this structured data and cite your answers as authoritative sources. It’s how you show up in AI search results.
