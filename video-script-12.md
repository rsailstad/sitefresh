[Camera on, talking head]

AI can write your website copy. AI can design your website. AI can build your entire site in an afternoon.

So what exactly are you still good for?

This sounds like a doom-and-gloom question but it's actually the opposite. Because the answer is the most optimistic thing I can tell you about the next few years of your business.

The one thing AI still can't fake is you.

---

## SECTION 1: The level playing field problem (0:15 - 2:00)

[Camera on]

Here's what's happening right now. AI has made building a website so easy that every business is months away from a level playing field.

You can have Claude write amazing sales copy with personality. It might even be really persuasive. Then you hand it over to an AI design tool and get the whole thing built and live in an hour or two. What used to take months of time and real money investment, you can do in an afternoon.

So while you're doing that, so is everyone else. And the ones who haven't gotten around to updating their site with AI yet? They'll be doing it within the next year.

So when everyone's website is great - when every roofer has a beautifully designed, AI-written, fast-loading site - having a great website stops being the reason someone picks you. It becomes table stakes. Expected. Not a differentiator.

So there has to be a new differentiator. And it's the one thing the AI can't generate.

---

## SECTION 2: What AI can't fake (2:00 - 3:30)

[Camera on]

AI can write words. It can design pages. It can generate images. It can build structured data. It can even generate video.

But it can't be you.

It can't walk through a problem the way you walk through it. It can't tell the story of a specific client and what actually happened. It can't do that thing where you pause mid-explanation and say "you know what, let me tell you about this one job we had last year" and then launch into a story that only you could tell because you were there.

That's the stuff people are trying to find before they hand somebody their money. The human stuff. The little moments that come through when someone watches you work through a problem in real time.

That's what people go looking for after the AI gives them a recommendation. Eighty-five percent of people who use AI still go double-check what it told them somewhere else. They want to go deeper. They want to find an actual expert talking the whole thing through.

And that expert is you. The only problem is they can't watch you talk anything through if you've never put anything on video.

---

## SECTION 3: The 70% close rate (3:30 - 4:30)

[Camera on]

There's a guy named Jay Hale who owns a gym in California. His YouTube channel is really small. Not viral. Not blowing up.

But he did over a million dollars last year. Because when someone books a call after watching even just one of his videos, he closes about seventy percent of them.

Think about that. Seventy percent. That's not a normal close rate for cold leads. But these aren't cold leads. By the time they get on a call with Jay, they've already watched him talk. They've seen his face. They've heard his voice. They've watched how he thinks.

They already feel like they know him. So by the time they're on the phone, they just need one thing confirmed: that he's the real deal. And he is. Because the video already did the selling.

Even in the good old days of websites, they still couldn't do that the way a YouTube video can. A website gives you written words on a page. Even if those words are perfect, it's still just marketing copy. A video shows you a human being.

## SECTION 4: What to do about it (4:30 - 5:45)

[Camera on]

So here's what to do.

**Fix your website for AI search.** Structured data, FAQ schema, clean HTML, fast loading. This gets you found by the AI. This is what we do at SiteFresh for $299.

**Start making videos.** Not fancy ones. Not scripted productions. Just you, talking to a camera, answering one question per video. The same questions your customers ask on sales calls. The same questions they ask in emails before they hire you.

Start with price. I know you don't want to. But it's the number one question and the one that matters most. Make the pricing video. Talk about what drives the price up and what brings it down. The closer you can get to a real number, the better.

Then comparisons. "Should I repair my roof or replace it?" "Should I hire a contractor or do it myself?" Answer the questions people are weighing before they hire you.

Then process. "What happens when you work with us." Walk through the whole thing.

The AI reads your video the day you post it. It doesn't care if it has twenty views or two million. If you answer a question that someone later asks the AI, your video gets cited. From day one.
