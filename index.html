What is AI search optimization?
AI search optimization means structuring your website so that AI search engines - Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, and others - can read it, understand it, and cite it as an authoritative source. This includes JSON-LD structured data (FAQ schema, service schema, business schema), semantic HTML, Open Graph tags, and clean content that answers real questions. It's the 2026 version of SEO, designed for how people actually search now.

What is structured data / schema?
Structured data is code embedded in your website that tells search engines exactly what your content means, not just what it says. We use JSON-LD (the format Google recommends) to mark up your business info, services, prices, FAQ, and hours. Google, ChatGPT, and Perplexity use this kind of clean, machine-readable information to understand who you are and what you offer. One honest note: Google stopped showing FAQ drop-downs in regular results for most businesses in 2023, so the value today is being understood correctly by AI tools, not a special search listing.

AI website builders (Wix ADI, Framer AI, etc.) generate a site that looks fine but isn't structured for AI search.
