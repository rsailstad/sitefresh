## SECTION 3: What this means for patients (4:00 - 5:30)

[Camera on]

Here's what happens when a patient in Columbus asks ChatGPT "what's the best dentist near me that takes Delta Dental?"

The AI tries to read every dentist's website in Columbus. It looks for two things: LocalBusiness schema that says "I'm a dentist, here's where I am" and a property called insuranceAccepted that says "I take Delta Dental."

None of the ten sites I audited had either.

So what does the AI do? It falls back to aggregator sites. Zocdoc. Healthgrades. Aspen Dental. These sites have structured data, insurance information, location data - all machine-readable. The AI cites them.

So when a patient asks about a Columbus dentist, the AI recommends Zocdoc's list or Aspen Dental - a chain - instead of the independent practice down the street. Not because the chain is better. Because the chain's website is readable and the independent's isn't.

The patient books with whoever the AI recommended. The independent dentist never even knew they were considered.

## SECTION 4: What it would take to fix (5:30 - 7:00)

[Screen share: sitefresh.co/get-started]

Here's what we'd fix for each of these ten dentists. And this is the same thing we do for any small business:

**Add LocalBusiness and Dentist schema.** Name, address, phone, hours, services, area served, price range. The AI instantly knows "this is a dentist in Columbus, here's what they do."

**Add FAQ schema with 10-20 questions.** "Do you take new patients?" "What insurance do you accept?" "How much is a cleaning without insurance?" "Do you do emergency dentistry?" "What are your hours?" Each one as a structured Q&A pair that the AI can cite.

**Add insuranceAccepted data.** A list of insurance providers the practice accepts, in the LocalBusiness schema. This is what the AI looks for when a patient asks "does this dentist take Delta Dental?"

**Add a dental insurance checker widget.** We built this - it's a widget where patients type their insurance provider and instantly see if the practice accepts it. No phone call needed. It captures leads when the insurance isn't listed. Uses the same insuranceAccepted property the AI reads.

**Optimize page speed.** Most of these sites loaded 200-400KB of assets. We'd get them under 50KB.
