What it does

Instant insurance lookup

Patients type their provider and instantly see if the practice accepts it. No phone call, no waiting on hold, no friction.

Lead capture built in

If the insurance isn't in the list, the widget asks for their email and opens a pre-filled email to the practice. Every unknown insurance becomes a lead.

Zero API calls

The insurance list is a local JSON file. No external services, no monthly fees, no vendor lock-in. Works offline. GDPR-safe by design.

AI search friendly

Uses the same insuranceAccepted property that AI search engines look for in Dentist and LocalBusiness schema. When patients ask "does Dr. X take Delta Dental?", your site is the answer.

Customizable

Every practice has a different insurance list. We configure the widget with your accepted providers at build time. Cash-only practices get a different variant.

Book appointment CTA

When insurance is accepted, the widget shows a "Book appointment" button that opens a pre-filled email to schedule. Frictionless conversion.
