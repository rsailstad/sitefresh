# Video 13 Script: "I asked ChatGPT for an accountant. Here's what happened."
## SiteFresh YouTube Channel · ~5-7 minutes

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 - 0:15)

[Camera on, talking head]

About a year ago I needed a new accountant. I didn't Google it. I asked ChatGPT.

And the way I searched tells you everything you need to know about how your customers are finding you now - and why "just rank in Google" doesn't work anymore.

---

## SECTION 1: The search (0:15 - 2:00)

[Camera on, or screen share showing a ChatGPT conversation]

Here's what I didn't do. I didn't type "accountant near me" into Google. I didn't scroll through ten blue links and read accountant websites one by one.

Here's what I did do. I opened ChatGPT and I told it everything. I said "I need an accountant who specializes in people like me who sell courses and are on YouTube. I want someone who already knows about my situation - what I can write off, how I can save money. I want someone in my corner who already understands all that."

I gave it my whole situation. Not two or three keywords. The whole context.

And ChatGPT gave me three names. Not ten links. Three names, each with a reason why they might be a good fit for my specific situation.

I set up meetings with all three. I hired the one I had the best conversation with. The one that I felt could actually help me the most.

Now, here's what's important about this. I didn't ask for a "good accountant." I asked for a specialist. Someone who lives and breathes in the course-seller and YouTube world. A generalist would never have been the answer to my question, no matter how good they were.

The AI didn't give me the best accountant. It gave me the most specific match for my situation.

---

## SECTION 2: Why this is your problem (2:00 - 3:30)

[Camera on]

That's how your customers are searching now. Not with keywords. With context. They're telling the AI their whole situation and asking for a curated short list.

And if your website is just a generic "we provide excellent service" page with no structured data, no FAQ schema, and no specific niche - you're never going to be on that short list.

Because here's what the AI is doing. It's reading your LocalBusiness schema to see what you do. It's reading your FAQ schema to see what questions you answer. It's reading your content to see who you specialize in. And if you don't specialize in anyone - if you're for everybody - there's no question that you're the obvious answer to.

When you're for everybody, there's no question you're the answer to. The specialist gets handed exactly the client they were built for. The generalist gets skipped.

---

## SECTION 3: The double-check (3:30 - 4:30)

[Camera on]

But here's the part that matters even more. The AI gave me three names. I didn't just pick one and hire them. I met with all three.

Fifty-three percent of people using AI say what they're mainly using it for is comparing their options and narrowing them down. The AI does that. Then the person goes and makes the call themselves.

But what are they making that call on? They're not stopping at the AI's answer. Eighty-five percent of people who use AI still go double-check what it told them somewhere else. They want to go deeper. They want to find an actual expert talking the whole thing through.

So after the AI gives them your name, they go looking for you. On YouTube. On your website. On your reviews. They're looking for the human behind the business. The person they'll feel safe hiring.

If they can find you on YouTube - talking through the exact problem they have - you're done. You're the one they call. Because they already feel like they know you.

If they can't find you on YouTube but they find your competitor? Your competitor gets the call.

## SECTION 4: The gutter guy (4:30 - 5:15)

[Camera on]

There's a guy named Jonathan in Tennessee who installs gutters. Almost all of his business comes through AI search now. Here's how.

He made YouTube videos. Gutter comparisons, tests, installation tutorials. Some people found those videos and hired him directly. But the AI models also watched those videos. They know what he specializes in. They know what area he works in.

So when somebody in Tennessee asks ChatGPT or Gemini or Claude who should install their gutter guards, his name comes up. And all those recommendations trace back to the videos.

He doesn't have a huge channel. He doesn't have viral videos. He just answered the questions his customers ask - on camera - and the AI found him.
