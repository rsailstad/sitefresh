# Video 11 Script: "Your website used to be your first impression. Now it's the last step."
## SiteFresh YouTube Channel · ~6-8 minutes

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 - 0:20)

[Camera on, talking head]

Your website used to be someone's first impression of your business.

Someone would Google "roofing contractor near me," click a link, land on your homepage, and form their opinion based on what they saw. Your design. Your copy. Your photos. That was the moment you won or lost them.

That's not how it works anymore.

Your website is now closer to the last step in the sales process. The discovery happened somewhere else. The confidence-building happened somewhere else. Your site is just where the deal closes.

Let me explain what changed and what it means for you.

---

## SECTION 1: Where discovery happens now (0:20 - 2:30)

[Camera on]

Here's what used to happen. Someone needed a roofer. They Googled it. They got ten blue links. They clicked the top three. They read each website. They compared. They picked one. Your website was their first impression.

Here's what happens now. Someone needs a roofer. They open ChatGPT and type "I need a roofing contractor in Minneapolis that handles insurance claims and works with insurance adjusters. My roof was damaged in a hail storm last week and I'm not sure if I should file a claim or pay out of pocket."

ChatGPT doesn't show them ten links. It reads the internet, synthesizes an answer, and gives them a short list of two or three roofers with reasons why each one might be a good fit.

That's where discovery happens now. Not on your website. In the AI.

Forty-three percent of people have used an AI assistant to research something they were about to buy. One in four people say AI is their main source for recommendations. That's ahead of going to a company's own website.

Your customer's first impression of your business isn't your homepage anymore. It's whatever the AI told them about you. And if the AI didn't tell them about you - because it couldn't read your website - your homepage never even entered the conversation.

---

## SECTION 2: Where confidence-building happens (2:30 - 4:00)

[Camera on]

So the AI gave them a short list. Two or three names. Now what?

They don't just take the AI's word for it. Eighty-five percent of people who use AI still go double-check what it told them somewhere else. They want to go deeper. They want to find an actual expert talking the whole thing through.

Where do they go? YouTube.

SimilarWeb tracked 1.3 billion clicks out of AI chatbots in a single month. The number one destination they went to next, right out of the AI chat, was YouTube. More than Facebook, Wikipedia, and Reddit combined. Three quarters of those clicks came from ChatGPT.

And here's what they're looking for on YouTube. Not your homepage. Not a list of services. They're looking for you. The human. The expert. The person who does this all day and can talk through the whole problem.

They want to see your face. Hear your voice. Watch how you think and how you go about the work. Because that's the stuff that makes them feel safe choosing you. Like they already know you.

A website can't do that. A website gives you written words on a page. Even if those words are perfect, it's still just marketing copy. And here's the thing - AI has made building a website so easy now that pretty much every business website is months away from being on a level playing field. You can have Claude write amazing sales copy and design the whole thing in an afternoon. So can every one of your competitors.

When everyone's website is great, having a great website stops being the reason someone picks you.

---

## SECTION 3: What your website still does (4:00 - 5:30)

[Camera on]

So does your website still matter? Yes. But its job is smaller.

Your website's job now is to not get in the way of the deal closing. The confidence already got built on YouTube. The discovery already happened in the AI. Your site just needs to:

**Make it easy to book, schedule, or contact you.** No friction. No pop-ups. No confusing navigation. One clear path to the next step.

**Have the structured data that feeds AI search.** JSON-LD, FAQ schema, LocalBusiness schema. So the AI can find you in the first place and cite you in its answer.

**Load fast and look good.** Especially now that AI makes every competitor's site look good too. You don't need a thirty-page mega-site. You need one fast, clean, structured landing page that converts.

**Not block the conversion.** No required sign-ups. No walls of text before the contact form. No "schedule a consultation" when they just want to send you a message.

Most businesses with a proper video strategy could go from a thirty-page mega-site down to a single landing page. Because the trust was already built before they arrived.

---

## SECTION 4: The new strategy (5:30 - 6:45)

[Camera on]

So here's the new strategy. Three pieces, not one.

**Piece one: your website.** Get it AI-search-optimized. Structured data, FAQ schema, clean HTML, fast loading. This is what gets you found by the AI. This is what SiteFresh does for $299.

**Piece two: your YouTube channel.** Answer the questions your customers ask. One question per video. Start with pricing - that's the one everyone wants to know and the one you're most afraid to answer. Then comparisons, process, timelines, how to choose. This is what builds the confidence that your website used to build. The AI reads your video the day you post it. It doesn't care about views or channel size.

**Piece three: your niche.** When you're for everybody, there's no question you're the answer to. But when you're the roofing contractor who specializes in hail damage and insurance claims - that's a specific question the AI can answer with your name. "I need a roofer in Minneapolis who handles insurance claims" - that's not a generalist query. That's a specialist query. And the AI picks the specialist.

Your website feeds the AI the data. Your videos feed it the trust. Your niche makes you the specific answer to a specific question. All three work together.
